Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Subscription billing

OptPoP Pro is a recurring subscription billed in advance (monthly) through Stripe. It renews automatically until you cancel.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime from Manage subscription in the app, which opens the Stripe billing portal. When you cancel, your Pro access continues until the end of the current paid period, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

Because you can cancel at any time and keep access through the period you already paid for, we generally do not provide prorated refunds for partial periods. If you were charged in error or believe there is a problem, contact us within 7 days of the charge at michael.colgan@gmail.com and we will review it in good faith.

Purchases made through an app store

If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, billing, cancellations, and refunds are handled by that store under its policies, and you should manage the subscription through your store account.
