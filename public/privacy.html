Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OptPoP, provided by Pera Pera, Inc. ("we," "us"), handles information. OptPoP is designed to keep your data on your device wherever possible.

Information stored on your device

Your strategy inputs, app settings, subscription state, and any market-data API token you enter are stored locally in your browser/app (via local storage) on your device. We do not transmit or store them on our servers.

Market data

If you connect a market-data provider (e.g., Tradier), your API token and requests go directly from your device to that provider under your own account. We never receive, proxy, or store your token or the returned data.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, information you enter (such as email and payment details) is collected and processed by Stripe under its privacy policy; we do not receive or store your card number. To manage your subscription we use a Stripe customer identifier associated with your device.

Advertising & cookies

The free version of OptPoP displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and may collect information such as your device and usage data and (for personalized ads) prior visits to this and other sites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.
  • Subscribing to OptPoP Pro removes ads.

Analytics

We do not run our own analytics or tracking beyond what is described above.

Children

OptPoP is not directed to children and is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can clear locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser/app storage for OptPoP. You can disconnect market data by removing your token in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.
