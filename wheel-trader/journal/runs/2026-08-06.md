## Calibration finding (useful for future runs)
A ~110% strike LEAPS costs roughly:
- **9–11% of share price** on low-IV underlyings (16–20% IV: the ETFs)
- **~27% of share price** on high-IV underlyings (CLF at 66% IV)

So a $182 budget supports underlyings priced up to **~$18–20** (low IV) or
**~$7** (high IV). Every sub-$20 name in scan 1 failed on trend or yield
today. The ETF universe is structurally out of reach until the account is
roughly **$1,500–2,200** (XLB-class) or **$2,200+** (XLE-class).
